Which practice fits

Astrology, tarot, or psychic—depending on the angle

Life-direction questions are big. Different practices read different parts of them.

If your question is…Best fitWhy
“What are my tendencies and life themes?”Astrology (natal)A birth chart is a symbolic map of long-arc patterns
“When does this chapter shift?”Astrology (transits)Timing is astrology's core strength
“Two paths—which one fits me?”TarotStructured reflection on a real decision within the arc
“What's blocking me right now?”TarotSurfaces the pattern under a stuck feeling
“A specific person's role in my direction?”PsychicDirect outside read on a person's influence
“I just want to start free”Daily CardLowest-stakes entry to reflective practice

Quick rule

Long-arc tendencies or timing → astrology. A decision within the arc → tarot. A specific person's influence → psychic. If you can't tell, take Do What Fits.
