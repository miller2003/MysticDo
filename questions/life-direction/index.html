“What's my purpose? What's next? Where is this going?” Life-arc questions are where astrology earns its place—the long view, the tendencies, the timing of chapters.

Direct answer

For life-arc questions—purpose, direction, transitions—astrology (natal + transit) is the strongest fit. It's built for the long view. If you also want reflection on a specific decision within that arc, add tarot. Take the astrology quiz to match the right reading type.

Key takeaways

  • Astrology is built for the long arc. Natal for tendencies, transits for chapter timing.
  • You need your birth time for a serious natal reading—the quiz flags this.
  • Tarot adds reflection on a specific decision within the larger arc.
