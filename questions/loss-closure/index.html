Intent · Loss & Closure

You're navigating loss

This intent deserves particular care. A medium reading is one tool—not a substitute for grief support. Here's what fits and when to wait.

Direct answer

If you're in early, acute grief and without support, a medium is not the next step—a grief counselor or support group is. If some time has passed and you're seeking connection or meaning, a medium reading may fit. Take the medium quiz to check fit and stage.

Key takeaways

  • A medium is for connection, not crisis care. If you're in acute grief, build support first.
  • The medium category has the highest fraud risk. Vetting bar is higher than other categories.
  • Responsibility matters more than “accuracy.” A medium who refuses to guarantee contact is more trustworthy than one who does.
