Which practice fits

Medium, psychic, tarot—or grief support first

Loss is the one intent where the wrong choice costs more than money. Stage and support matter before practice.

Quick rule

Acute grief → support first, not a reading. Connection with someone who passed → medium. Meaning of the loss for you → tarot. When in doubt, take the medium quiz—it flags stage and support before fit.

If you're in acute grief

A medium is meaning-making, not crisis care. If you're in early, acute grief and without support, please consider a licensed grief counselor or a support group first—they'll still be there when you're ready for a medium, and the medium will read better when you're resourced. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, contact a crisis line now: in the US, dial or text 988; in the UK, call 116 123 (Samaritans); elsewhere, see your national emergency number.
