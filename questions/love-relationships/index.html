Which practice fits

Psychic, tarot, or astrology—depending on the question

Same intent, different practices answer different angles of it.

If your question is…Best fitWhy
“What does this person feel / want right now?”PsychicDirect outside read on a specific person
“Will they reach out?”PsychicSpecific question about a person's near-term move
“What's the dynamic between us?”TarotStructured reflection on a relationship dynamic
“Why do I keep repeating this pattern?”TarotPattern-spotting, not a person-read
“Are we compatible long-term?”Astrology (synastry)Two charts describe long-term patterns
“When will my love life shift?”Astrology (transits)Timing is astrology's core strength
“Someone passed and I have unfinished things”MediumConnection with someone who passed—see Loss & Closure

Quick rule

Direct read on the person now → psychic. Pattern or dynamic → tarot. Long-term compatibility or timing → astrology. If you can't tell, take Do What Fits.
