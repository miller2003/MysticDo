Which practice fits

Astrology, psychic, or tarot—depending on the question

Honest framing: timing and pattern questions fit spiritual guidance. Specific financial decisions need financial advice first—spiritual perspective comes second.

If your question is…Best fitWhy
“When's the window for money growth?”Astrology (transits)2nd-house / 8th-house timing
“What's my money pattern / tendency?”Astrology (natal)Birth chart describes tendencies
“Should I take this specific financial step?”Financial advisor firstSpiritual guidance can't verify the math
“Why do I keep sabotaging my finances?”TarotPattern reflection on money scripts
“Something feels off about this deal”PsychicOutside intuitive read on the situation

Honest limit

No psychic, astrologer, or tarot reader can predict market movements or validate the math of a financial decision. If your question is “should I invest / take this loan / take this deal,” consult a licensed financial advisor first. Spiritual guidance is for timing, patterns, and perspective—not for the numbers.
