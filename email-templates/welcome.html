What you'll get

— A decision guide. What things cost, how to tell legit from not, how to choose.
— A free tool or exercise. Five minutes, no signup.
— Occasionally, a plain-language note on how this industry actually works.

What you won't get

Daily horoscopes.

Lists of affiliate offers dressed up as advice.

Anything that pretends to know your future.
