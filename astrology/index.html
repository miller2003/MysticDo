Astrology Readings

Astrology is best for big-picture patterns, timing, and compatibility — the arcs of a life, when to act, how two people's charts interact. It is not a daily horoscope. A real reading uses your full birth chart, not your sun sign.

What it is

An astrology reading, in one paragraph

An astrology reading interprets your natal chart — the positions of planets at your exact birth time and place — to describe tendencies, life themes, and timing. A serious reading is bespoke to your chart, not your sun-sign column. It gives you a symbolic map of your patterns, not a forecast.

The short version

Astrology fits when you want timing, long-arc patterns, or compatibility. It does not fit for direct reads on a specific person's current state (try psychic) or for reflective pattern-spotting within one situation (try tarot).

How does a paid reading differ from your daily horoscope? See Astrology Reading vs Horoscope.

Who it's for

It fits when…

  • You want timing — when a window opens, when something is likely to resolve.
  • You're working with compatibility — how two people's charts interact over time.
  • You're at a life-arc crossroads — career, vocation, purpose over years, not days.
  • You want a symbolic self-map — your tendencies, blind spots, themes.

It does not fit when…

  • You want a direct intuitive read on a specific person now. Try psychic.
  • You want to reflect on one situation's dynamics. Try tarot.
  • You want to connect with someone who passed. Try a medium.

Cost

What an astrology reading costs

Key takeaways

  • Natal readings from serious astrologers run $80–$250 for a 60–90 min session.
  • Transit readings are often shorter and cheaper — $60–$180.
  • App-generated charts are free — but the interpretation is what you're paying for.
  • Astrology rewards repeat sessions — a one-off natal reading is a map; transits are the live updates.

Price ranges are indicative. Verify current pricing on the astrologer's site.

How to choose

How to choose an astrologer

  1. Confirm your birth time first — without it, the chart's houses are guesses. Check your birth certificate.
  2. Pick the right reading type — natal for self, transit for timing, synastry for relationships.
  3. Read how the astrologer frames astrology — psychological/symbolic astrologers and predictive astrologers give very different sessions.
  4. Avoid sun-sign-only astrologers — serious astrology uses the full chart, not just your sun sign.

Red flags

  • Doom transits — “Saturn return will destroy your relationship.”
  • Readings that can't explain the houses or aspects they're citing.
  • Predictive certainty about specific events (“you will meet someone on…”).
