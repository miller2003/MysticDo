What it is

A psychic reading, in one paragraph

A psychic reading is a session where a reader uses intuitive perception to respond to your questions — usually about people, decisions, or situations you can't fully see. The best frame is not “will tell me my future,” but “can give me a different angle on a question I'm stuck on.”

The short version

If you have a specific question and want an outside read on it, a psychic reading fits. If you want to understand patterns in your life over time, tarot or astrology fits better. If you want to connect with someone who has passed, you want a medium.

Who it's for

It fits when…

  • You have a specific question about a person, decision, or situation and want an outside read.
  • You're stuck between options and want a perspective you can't generate yourself.
  • You want conversational, responsive guidance rather than a static report.
  • You're navigating uncertainty about someone else (a relationship, an ex, a business partner).

It does not fit when…

  • You want timing — “when” something will happen. Astrology is built for that.
  • You want to reflect on your own patterns. Tarot is built for that.
  • You want to reach someone who passed. That's mediumship.

Cost

What a psychic reading costs

Key takeaways

  • Per-minute is the dominant model on platforms — typically $1–$15/min.
  • Free intro minutes are a marketing tool, not a free reading. Treat them as a sample.
  • Longer ≠ better. A focused 15-minute reading often beats an unstructured hour.
  • Per-session pricing from independent readers runs ~$60–$300 and tends to be more structured.

Most platforms price per-minute. The trap is not the per-minute rate itself — it's sessions that drift without a clear question, running up time. Decide your budget and question before connecting.

Price ranges reflect publicly listed platform pricing at time of writing and are indicative. Always verify current pricing on the provider's site.

How to choose

A fixed framework for choosing a psychic reader

Don't sort by 5-star averages. Use this sequence.

  1. Match specialization to your question — readers who specialize in relationships tend to read relationship questions better than generalists.
  2. Read a sample of their actual writing or voice — most platforms let you read a reader's intro. Vague, all-caps, or copy-pasted intros are a signal.
  3. Check the review distribution, not the average — 4.9 from 12 reviews tells you little; read the 3-stars for what went wrong.
  4. Set a time budget before connecting — 15 focused minutes is a fine first session.
  5. Bring one specific question, not five — the question does most of the work.

Red flags

  • Guaranteed outcomes, fixed-future claims, or “curse removal” upsells.
  • Pressure to extend the session beyond your stated budget.
  • No clear refund or satisfaction policy.

FAQ

Common questions

How much does a psychic reading cost?
Platform readings are typically $1–$15 per minute; independent readers charge $60–$300 per session. See cost above.
Are online psychics legit?
Some are; the question is how to tell. We cover red flags and verification in How to Choose a Psychic Reader.
How long does a reading take?
Most first sessions are 15–30 minutes. A focused short session usually beats an unstructured long one.
What should I ask?
One specific question beats five vague ones. The Question Generator (coming soon) helps sharpen vague worries into reading-ready questions.
Psychic vs medium — what's the difference?
A psychic responds to your questions about people and situations. A medium connects with someone who has passed. Not all psychics are mediums.
